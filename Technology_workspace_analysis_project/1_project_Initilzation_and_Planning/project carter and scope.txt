============================================================
PROJECT CHARTER: Comprehensive Analysis of Technology Sector Workforce
============================================================

1. PROJECT OBJECTIVES:
- To analyze recent workforce trends in the global technology sector.
- To evaluate the impact of AI adoption on hiring, retention, and restructuring.
- To understand market conditions leading to tech industry layoffs.
- To study employee sentiment variations across different roles and organizational updates.

2. PROJECT SCOPE:
- Target Industry: Technology Sector (Software, Hardware, AI, Cloud Services).
- Data Insights: Layoff counts, AI implementation rates, and employee satisfaction levels.
- Deliverables: Tableau interactive dashboards, fully formatted executive presentation story, and comprehensive final project report.

3. METHODOLOGY:
- Step 1: Project Initiation & Setup
- Step 2: Data Collection & Data Cleaning/Preprocessing
- Step 3: Exploratory Data Analysis & Visualization via Tableau
- Step 4: Building Interactive Multi-perspective Dashboards
- Step 5: Structuring Findings into a Final Executive Story
